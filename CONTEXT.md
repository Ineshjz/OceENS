# OcéEns

EPF's course evaluation platform: surveys are created per programme, students answer them, and the answers are exported, visualised and summarised.

## Language

### Where French stays

The project's working language is English: code identifiers, issues, ADRs and documentation. The product itself is French: its interface, its messages to users, and the survey content are written in French, for French-speaking users.

French therefore remains only for product text: interface labels, messages and survey content, quoted verbatim when a document refers to them (« Changer d'utilisateur »), and the French terms below when naming what a user sees on screen. Everything else in the documentation is English. Code comments and commit messages written in French before this boundary was drawn are not translated retroactively.

### Product vocabulary

**Survey** (*sondage*, `Survey`):
A survey template instantiated for one programme, school year and semester, which enrolled students answer.

**Summary** (*synthèse*, `Summary`):
A text generated by an LLM from the free-text answers to one question of a survey, for one module and teacher when the question is about one. Summaries are queued by the application and generated one at a time by the summaries daemon.
_Avoid_: synthesis, digest

**Free-text answer** (*verbatim*):
A student's written answer to an open question, as opposed to a choice among options. Summaries are made from these.

**Programme** (*filière*, *formation*, `Program`):
A course of study at EPF; the scope of a programme manager's and a facilitator's roles.

### Authentication

**Development sign-in** (*connexion de développement*, `AUTH_MODE=dev`):
Sign-in without an identity provider: you choose a user's e-mail address and are signed in as them, with no proof of identity. It only exists when `AUTH_MODE=dev` and must never be used in production.
_Avoid_: impersonation, fake login
